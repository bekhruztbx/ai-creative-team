# Relay: post copy, never the post itself

1. **Read** `out/<name>/notes.md` and `out/<name>/captions.srt`, and the brand rules (`mcp__memory__open_nodes` with names ["Brand rules"]).
2. **Write** `out/<name>/post.md` with:
   - **YouTube Shorts**: title (under 60 characters, the same promise as the cover), a 2-line description, 3 hashtags.
   - **Instagram**: a caption whose first line works on its own, then 2 to 3 short lines, then 5 hashtags at most.
   - **Pinned comment**: one question that invites a real answer.
3. **Check** every title against the brand rules (length, no clickbait).

End with: "Nothing is posted. Waiting for Bekhruz's OK."
